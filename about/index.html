About This Resource

A Comparable Pack method and cargo tag reading built around a shared MuleBuy spreadsheet.

What this resource is

This is an independent informational and navigation resource for people who use a MuleBuy spreadsheet. It offers a Comparable Pack method — same purpose, matched option, matched quantity, and known included parts — so each candidate group is aligned before any difference is discussed.

What this resource is not

This resource is not the official MuleBuy website, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by MuleBuy or Google. It is not a store, a link converter, a QC database, a price tracker, a shipping calculator, or an order tracking tool.

The Comparable Pack method

Everything here follows four fields: Same purpose (the use or category), Matched option (color, size, version, material), Matched quantity (single, set, or count), and Known included parts (explicit, image-displayed, or optional). Different sizes, colors, quantities, materials, sets, accessories, or packaging are never treated as the same option.

How to use it

  1. Open the shared Google Sheet to see the listing references.
  2. Browse the twelve product categories from the home page.
  3. Read the three guides before relying on any comparison.
  4. Align the fields and re-check the destination page.

Comparison example — not a live listing, shipment, or recommendation.
